# 400

Research on patterns of greatness across 400+ influential figures.

## Overview

An interactive research project analyzing common patterns among history's most influential people across science, technology, business, politics, arts, philosophy, and social reform. Includes geo-mapping, AI-generated analyses, and synthesis documents.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in your browser to explore the interactive visualizations, or browse the `research_*.md` files for domain-specific findings.
